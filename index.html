Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Jaisalmer stone carving craftsmanship and custom orders.

We work exclusively with 100% authentic, premium-grade Jaisalmer yellow golden sandstone (#jaisalmerstone). Renowned worldwide for its warm golden radiance, fine uniform grain, extreme weather durability, and suitability for intricate hand chiseling.

We craft all types of stone carvings: Window Jharokhas (overhang balconies), Mehrab arches, carved columns & pillars, perforated Jali screens, 3D stone wall murals, Bada Bagh style chhatri domes, entrance portals, decorative elephant sculptures, ansort pati mouldings, and custom temple carvings.
